## Rust crates and UI packages

- `third_party/licenses/rust-crates.txt` — license texts of every Rust crate compiled into the
  binaries, grouped by license, generated with `cargo about` (`about.toml`, `tools/about.hbs`).
- `third_party/licenses/ui-npm.txt` — license texts of the npm packages bundled into the user
  interface (React, react-dom, scheduler, lucide-react, Tauri JS API and plugins).

Both are regenerated by `python3 -I tools/gen_licenses.py`; CI fails if they are out of date, and
`cargo deny` enforces the allowed-license policy (`deny.toml`). Both files are installed with the
app under `licenses/`. Three crates are MPL-2.0 (file-level copyleft; their unmodified sources are
available from crates.io).

## Test-only tools (not redistributed)

- `pypdf` (BSD-3-Clause) generates the encrypted test fixture; `lopdf` (MIT) independently parses
  outputs in tests.
- `axe-core` (MPL-2.0) is injected into the app only during end-to-end accessibility tests; it is a
  development dependency and is not part of any installer.
